## Regulatory program analysis

What a mandate will actually produce in a given organization or region — as
opposed to what it requires on paper. Grounded in the research program this
practice publishes: disclosure rules, reporting requirements, and
infrastructure programs, analyzed through the organizational capacity that
determines their outcomes.

## Governance assessment

Where an organization's rules and its behavior have come apart, and why.
Shadow IT, insider risk, and AI governance — the areas where policy travels
top-down as a document while behavior travels peer-to-peer as a habit.

## Engagement shapes

Two forms, both bounded:

- **Fixed-scope assessment.** A defined question, a defined period, a written
  finding. Scoped and priced before work begins.
- **Commissioned analysis.** Data work on a question you name — sources
  documented, method stated, and the result delivered whether or not it is
  the result anyone hoped for.

Findings are what the evidence supports. They are not negotiable, and no
outcome is promised in advance.
