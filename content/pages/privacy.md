## What this site collects

**Nothing, directly.** This site has no contact form, sets no cookies, runs
no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts or widgets, and loads no
resources from third-party servers — fonts and all other assets are served
from this domain. There is nothing to consent to, which is why there is no
consent banner.

**Email.** If you contact Threshold Data Sciences by email, your message —
your address and what you write — is handled by ordinary email systems and
is used for exactly one purpose: reading and replying to your inquiry.

**Server logs.** The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub, like all web
hosts, keeps standard short-lived server logs (requested URLs, timestamps,
IP addresses) for operating and securing the service; its practices are
described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Threshold Data Sciences does not receive or use those logs to identify
visitors.

## Retention

Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the inquiry
and any engagement that follows from it, and is then deleted.

## Your requests

To ask what correspondence Threshold Data Sciences holds from you, or to
have it deleted, write to the address on the [contact page](/contact/).
Deletion requests are honored.

## Changes

If the site's data practices ever change — for example, if a privacy-
respecting page counter is added — this page will be updated first, with the
"Updated" date reflecting the change.
