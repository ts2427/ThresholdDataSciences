## What this site collects

**The contact form.** If you use the [contact form](/contact/), the site
collects what you type into it: your name, your email address, and your
message. That information is transmitted to Threshold Data Sciences by the
site's hosting provider's form-handling service and is used for exactly one
purpose — reading and replying to your inquiry.

**Server logs.** The hosting provider that serves this site keeps standard,
short-lived server logs (requested URLs, timestamps, IP addresses) for
operating and securing the service, as all web hosts do. Threshold Data
Sciences does not use these logs to identify visitors.

**Nothing else.** This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no
third-party scripts or widgets, and loads no resources from third-party
servers — fonts and all other assets are served from this domain. There is
nothing to consent to, which is why there is no consent banner.

## Retention

Form submissions are kept only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and
any engagement that follows from it, and are then deleted.

## Your requests

To ask what information Threshold Data Sciences holds from your form
submissions, or to have it deleted, write to the address on the
[contact page](/contact/). Deletion requests are honored.

## Changes

If the site's data practices ever change — for example, if a privacy-
respecting page counter is added — this page will be updated first, with the
"Updated" date below reflecting the change.
