## What this site collects

**The contact form.** If you use the [contact form](/contact/), the site
collects what you type into it: your name, your email address, and your
message. The submission is transmitted by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a
form-relay service, which forwards it to Threshold Data Sciences by email.
It is used for exactly one purpose — reading and replying to your inquiry.
FormSubmit's own handling is described in its
[privacy policy](https://formsubmit.co/privacy).

**Server logs.** The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub, like all web
hosts, keeps standard short-lived server logs (requested URLs, timestamps,
IP addresses) for operating and securing the service; its practices are
described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Threshold Data Sciences does not receive or use those logs to identify
visitors.

**Nothing else.** This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no
scripts, fonts, or other resources from third-party servers. There is
nothing to consent to, which is why there is no consent banner.

## Retention

Contact-form submissions and the email correspondence that follows are kept
only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any engagement that follows
from it, and are then deleted.

## Your requests

To ask what information Threshold Data Sciences holds from your submissions,
or to have it deleted, write via the [contact page](/contact/). Deletion
requests are honored.

## Changes

If the site's data practices ever change — for example, if a privacy-
respecting page counter is added — this page will be updated first.
