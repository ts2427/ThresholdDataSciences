## The target

This site targets [WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) Level AA. It
works without JavaScript, keeps text contrast at 4.5:1 or better, and gives
every chart an adjacent data table. Changes are checked with an automated
audit (axe-core) and by hand.

## Reporting a problem

If something on this site is hard to use with your browser, screen reader or
other assistive technology, email
[timothy@thresholddatasciences.com](mailto:timothy@thresholddatasciences.com)
with the page address and what went wrong, and I'll fix it.
