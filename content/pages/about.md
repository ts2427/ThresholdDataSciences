## The founder

I'm Timothy D. Spivey. I have doctoral training in business analytics, and
I've spent nearly twenty years in telecom and cable operations — building
reporting pipelines, sitting in escalation calls, and watching compliance
decisions get made under real pressure. That operational history is why the
questions I take on are the ones operators recognize, and why the analysis
here reads differently from work done at a distance.

I've published two articles in the ISACA Journal, on security culture and
shadow IT governance, and I hold an MBA in business analytics from the
University of West Florida. For the academic record — portfolio, teaching
and CV — see my [personal site](https://timothydspivey.com); this site is
the practice's.

## The name

The name comes from econometrics: a threshold effect is a relationship that
changes character once a variable crosses a critical value. Most of the
questions I take on live on lines like that.

## Independence

I have no vendor partnerships, take no referral fees, and my fee never
depends on what the analysis finds. If an engagement requires a
predetermined conclusion, I'll decline it. Threshold Data Sciences is not
affiliated with or endorsed by any university or employer, and public work
uses public data only.
