## How an engagement runs

1. **Fit call** — 20 minutes, free.
2. **Scope** — a written question, method, data needed, assumptions,
   deliverables, timeline, what is out of scope, and a fixed price, within
   three business days.
3. **Data** — a mutual NDA on request; access limited to what the question
   needs.
4. **Midpoint check-in** — early findings and any course correction.
5. **Handover** — a written report, the reproducible code and a data
   dictionary, a 60-minute readout, and 30 days of follow-up questions.

## What you receive

The report states the question, the method, the assumptions and the limits
in plain language. The code and data dictionary are yours on final payment.

## Standards

Analysis plans are written before results are seen. Every headline number
comes with its uncertainty. Robustness checks are reported, including the
ones that weaken the result. A null result is a result. Findings are what
the evidence supports; they are not negotiable, and no outcome is promised
in advance.

## Data handling

Client data is used only for the engagement, stored encrypted, never used in
public work, and deleted or returned at the end unless agreed otherwise in
writing.

## AI use

I use AI tools for drafting and code assistance. Every number, method choice
and conclusion is mine and checked by me. Client data is never entered into
AI tools without written permission.

## Questions

- **Why a fixed price?** You know the cost before work starts; any change in
  scope is priced in writing first.
- **Who owns the code?** You do, on final payment.
- **What if the data can't answer the question?** You'll hear that at
  scoping or the midpoint, not at delivery.
