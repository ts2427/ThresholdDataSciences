# Analysis plan addendum, version 2

**Exploratory: added after the version 1 results were published.** Version 1 (the page live on 2026-11-23) was
built under `docs/analysis_plan.md`. Everything below was written after those results were public, so none of it is
pre-registered in the v1 sense: results from it are exploratory and the page labels them so.

Written 2026-11-23, before any v2 quantity was computed. The thresholds marked **(proposed)** are Claude Code's
proposals for Tim's approval at checkpoint B1; once approved they are fixed, and any later change is logged in
`docs/decisions.md` as a deviation with its reason, before it is run.

## Rules that hold for every item

- **Sources.** Every number comes from `output/results.json` (new keys `v2_*`; the v1 chapter keys are not changed)
  or the published panel (`dirs_county_day.csv`), and is generated by code, including any summary line in the
  opening or on the Work page. A test checks that the v1 keys of `results.json` are byte-identical before and after
  each v2 step; any change to v1 analysis or results is asked about first.
- **Scope.** Cell sites only. No county-level wireline or cable analysis.
- **Time.** Day 0 is the v1 rule (first time 34-kt winds reach a study-state county). The time-flag rule holds:
  Laura's Aug 25, 2020 combined Marco–Laura report stays out of anything that places it on Laura's timeline and in
  wherever only same-time comparisons are made. Excluded reports are not used.
- **Censoring.** Times use the v1 rebound rule (the first report from which a value stays past the threshold in every
  later report that includes it); a county that leaves the reports while past the threshold is censored, not
  counted as recovered.
- **Wording.** Descriptive and associational only. No causal language ("causes", "leads to", "because of"), nothing
  implying intent or neglect, no provider named, inferred or estimated.
- **Exhibits.** Site palette (Ink and Slate for data; Signal only for rule-period markers and highlights). Each chart
  has a title, a text description and a data table; `a11y_audit.py` stays at 0 violations; the Medicaid and OSHA
  figures stay unchanged (snapshot test). Nothing on the page names who proposed what; the page's "Claude" guard
  applies.
- **Downloads.** Approved sources only, same terms (generic User-Agent, at most one request every 3 seconds, stop on
  403 or 429, ask before expanding a download, ask before anything over 1 GB, log every download).

## V2-1. What the public record misses

- **Question.** When the FCC's reports for a storm end, how many cell sites are still out, and where?
- **Outcome.** For each storm, from its last used report: sites out (count), that count as a share of the storm's
  peak sites out (v1 chapter 3 peak), and the counties with sites out > 0 in that report (county, state, sites out,
  sites served). Separately, counties that left the reports earlier while still out (v1 censoring
  "left_reports_while_out"), with their last reported sites out and date.
- **Method.** Counts and shares from the panel; no model. Storms in date order.
- **Thresholds.** "Still out" = sites out > 0. None other.
- **Sensitivity displays.** (a) Without reports carrying a serious validation flag. (b) Treating counties that left the
  reports while out as still out at the last report (their last reported value carried forward) versus counting only
  counties listed in the last report.
- **Wording limits.** "Still out in the FCC's last report", never "never restored" or "abandoned". Nicole's last report
  is its peak, so its share is 100% by construction; footnoted. Shares of the peak are not shares of sites served.

## V2-2. Backup power

- **Question.** How many cell sites were operating on backup power, by day and storm, where the FCC collected it?
- **Outcome.** For each storm and report: sites on backup power (sum over counties) and that count as a share of
  sites served in the counties that report it.
- **Method.** "Collected" = the report prints the on-backup-power column; a county row with a blank value counts as
  not collected, never as zero. Days since day 0 on the x axis (time-flag rule applies). Storms without the column
  are listed as "not collected".
- **Thresholds.** None.
- **Sensitivity displays.** Share of sites served over all the report's counties versus only counties with a printed
  value.
- **Wording limits.** Always "sites operating on backup power". Never "about to fail", "at risk", "predicted
  failures" or any statement about how long backup lasts. No link to later outages is claimed.

## V2-3. Power vs. cell timing

- **Question.** In each county, does the share of electricity customers without power fall below a threshold before,
  at the same report as, or after the share of cell sites out does?
- **Outcome.** Per storm-county: (a) the report time at which power outages fall below the power threshold, (b) the
  report time at which cell outages fall below the cell threshold, both in days since day 0 by the rebound rule, on
  the same report grid (EAGLE-I matched at each DIRS report's as-of time, as in v1 chapter 6). Order: power first,
  same report, cell first, or undetermined (censoring prevents ordering). Lag = (b) − (a) in days where both occur.
- **Thresholds (proposed; approved by Tim 2026-11-23 at B1).** Cell: below 5% of sites served (as in v1 chapter 8). Power: below 5% of EAGLE-I modeled
  customers. At risk on a measure: at or above its threshold in at least one report. A county enters only if it is at
  risk on both measures.
- **Method.** Counts and shares of the four order categories over storm-counties, with a storm bootstrap 95%
  interval (2,000 draws, seed 20261122); median lag with IQR among counties where both occur. Ordering under
  censoring: if one measure crosses and the other is censored at a later time, the order is known; if the censoring
  time comes first, "undetermined". Storms: those with EAGLE-I (2017–2025); Isaias 2026 is excluded (no EAGLE-I).
- **Sensitivity displays.** (a) Thresholds of 10% for both. (b) First crossing instead of the rebound rule. (c) Without
  low-coverage and coverage-unknown EAGLE-I counties. (d) Without reports carrying a serious flag. (e) By rule period,
  shown but not compared.
- **Wording limits.** "Power outages fell below 5% before cell outages did in X of Y counties"; no "power restoration
  restores cell sites", "drives" or "explains". Both series are snapshots at report times, so "same report" means
  within one report interval, not simultaneous. EAGLE-I "customers" are meters.

## V2-4. County lookup

- **Question.** For any county in the panel, what did each storm's outage and recovery look like there?
- **Outcome.** A selector of every county in the published panel; for the chosen county, one line per storm of
  % of sites out by days since day 0, with sites out and served in a data table.
- **Method.** Generated in the browser from the published panel CSV on the same site (no other server). Excluded
  reports are absent from the panel; the time-flagged Laura report is left off Laura's line (timeline rule) and noted.
  Gaps longer than one day are not interpolated (points, joined only between consecutive reports).
- **Thresholds.** None.
- **Sensitivity displays.** None (a lookup of the published data).
- **Wording limits.** Labels describe the FCC's reports only ("% of the county's cell sites out in this report").
  No ranking of counties, no "worst county" language.

## V2-5. Network redundancy

**Status: planned** (2026-11-23, reinstated). Run first as 'not run' because access could not be confirmed; reinstated after Tim set up a personal FCC account and token (kept in .env, never printed, logged or committed) and approved reading the specification documents. Provider counts are per county, for fixed and mobile broadband separately; providers are never named; the tercile cut points are fixed before any recovery data is joined.

- **Question.** Is recovery associated with how many mobile providers serve a county?
- **Gate.** Before any download: check whether the FCC's Broadband Data Collection (BDC) public data API allows
  programmatic access (documentation, terms of use, whether a token is needed, rate limits, and robots.txt, which
  returned 403 in v1). Report the findings to Tim and wait for approval. If access is not allowed, the item is
  dropped and the page says so.
- **Outcome (if approved).** Per county: the count of mobile broadband providers in the BDC release nearest to each
  storm (counts only; providers are never named or inferred). Recovery: v1 chapter 8's waiting time to stay below 5%
  of sites out.
- **Thresholds (proposed; replaced 2026-11-23, see the change below).** Redundancy levels: tertiles of the provider count across at-risk storm-counties, fixed
  before outcomes are joined.
- **Method.** Kaplan–Meier median waiting time by level with county-bootstrap 95% intervals; the v1 chapter 8 hazard
  model with the redundancy level added (storm fixed effects, county-clustered intervals), shown beside the raw
  table. BDC begins in 2022: storms before then use the earliest release.
- **Sensitivity displays.** (a) Only storms with a BDC release within 12 months. (b) Provider count as a number
  instead of tertiles. (c) Without the time-flagged report (timeline rule) and without serious-flag reports.
- **Wording limits.** "Counties served by more providers fell below 5% sooner / later / no measurable difference";
  no claim that more providers produce faster recovery; provider counts reflect availability, not which networks
  were out.

- **Changes made 2026-11-23, before any recovery data is joined (Tim's instruction).** (1) The main analysis covers only storms from 2022 on, each matched to the release nearest its date; storms before 2022, matched to the earliest release, appear only as a labelled sensitivity display, "network data from after the storm". (2) After download and before the tercile cut points are set, the distributions of fixed and mobile provider counts per county are reported (counts only, no outcomes). If the any-coverage mobile count barely varies across counties, "providers covering at least half the county's area" becomes the main mobile measure and any-coverage the sensitivity display. The main measures are proposed to Tim with the distributions, and the cut points are fixed only after his approval.

- **Change made 2026-11-23, before any download (Tim's instruction).** Each storm is matched to the latest BDC release on or before its day 0, not the nearest release, so the data describes the network the storm met: 2022-06-30 for Ian and Nicole (and the pre-2022 sensitivity storms), 2023-06-30 for Idalia, 2024-06-30 for Beryl, Debby, Francine, Helene and Milton, 2026-06-30 for Isaias. Four files.

- **Change made 2026-11-23, from counts only, before any recovery data was joined (Tim's decision).** Main measure: mobile providers covering at least half the county's area (any coverage barely varied); groups on whole values as close to thirds as ties allow, none under 20% of storm-counties (2 or fewer / 3 / 4 or more), replacing tertiles; fixed broadband, with near-universal providers excluded, only as sensitivity displays; measures that cannot form three groups use the count as a number.

## V2-6. Interactive map

- **Question.** Where were sites out on each report day of each storm?
- **Outcome.** A county map of the study states, shaded by % of sites out in the chosen storm's chosen report.
- **Method.** D3 and topojson-client served from this site (vendored into `static/js/`, with their licences); county
  shapes from the Census 2022 cartographic boundary file already downloaded, simplified and saved as TopoJSON for the
  ten study states. No map tiles and no request to any other server, so the privacy page stays true. Controls: storm
  selector, day slider over that storm's used reports, optional play button. Counties not in that day's report: a
  neutral "not reported" fill, never zero. Hover or tap: county, % out, sites out and served, and cause shares where
  collected. Keyboard: every control and county is reachable; a text description and the day's data table are the
  alternative. Works on a phone. The time-flagged Laura report is not a slider step (timeline rule).
- **Thresholds.** None (colour scale fixed across storms, 0 to the largest county share in the panel).
- **Sensitivity displays.** None.
- **Wording limits.** Descriptive labels only.

- **Changes made 2026-11-23 (Tim's decisions).** The colour scale is fixed steps for every storm (0 / under 5 / 5-15 / 15-30 / 30-50 / 50+ %), chosen from the panel's overall distribution, replacing "0 to the largest county share"; counties not in a report are hatched light grey, and 0% has a faint tint. The map is the page's first exhibit.
